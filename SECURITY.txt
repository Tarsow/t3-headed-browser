# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.

Report them privately to:

  tarsow@tarsow.dev

Include a clear description, steps to reproduce, and (if available) a
suggested fix.

## Response

We aim to acknowledge reports within a few days and to keep you informed of
progress.

## Disclosure

Please allow a reasonable window before public disclosure so a fix can be
released. We will credit reporters who follow responsible disclosure.
